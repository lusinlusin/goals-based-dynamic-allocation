# Data source

`us_annual_returns_1928_2025.csv` — annual total returns in percent, 1928–2025.

| column | meaning |
|---|---|
| `sp500` | S&P 500 including dividends |
| `tbill` | 3-month US Treasury bill |
| `tbond` | 10-year US Treasury bond |

Source: Aswath Damodaran (NYU Stern), *Historical Returns on Stocks, Bonds and Bills: 1928–2025*,
<https://pages.stern.nyu.edu/~adamodar/New_Home_Page/datafile/histretSP.html>, retrieved 2026-11-10.
Returns are nominal.
